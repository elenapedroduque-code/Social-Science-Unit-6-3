🚨 What is Happening to Earth?

Nowadays, changes in climate are happening very, very fast! Why? Because of certain destructive human activities.

These actions are increasing the levels of carbon dioxide in the Earth’s atmosphere. This traps heat and causes a rapid increase in temperature. We call this... GLOBAL WARMING! 🔥
